⌑ On the method

Design thinking is the operating model, not as a workshop format but as a genuine analytical discipline. Every engagement, whether commercial or technical, starts with the same question: what is the actual problem, and what is the simplest thing that solves it correctly?

At DSV, that meant modular pricing catalogues and governance frameworks instead of bespoke per-customer setups. At Lake-Project, it means scoped, governed SaaS products instead of all-in-one platforms that nobody can run without the builder in the room.

The constraint is the same in both cases: the output has to be handed over to operations and survive without ongoing involvement.
